# Miter

> Miter is a Human Capital Management (HCM) platform purpose-built for construction contractors, unifying payroll, HR, benefits, field operations, and expense management. Its construction payroll automates prevailing-wage, certified-payroll, and union compliance, multi-state taxes, and real-time fully-burdened job costing. Miter integrates with construction ERPs and accounting systems and exposes a gated REST API for custom integrations. Backed by Bessemer Venture Partners.

## Products
- [Human Capital Management](https://www.miter.com/human-capital-management/): Payroll, HRIS, benefits, recruiting, performance, and learning
- [Construction Payroll](https://www.miter.com/human-capital-management/payroll/): Certified payroll, prevailing wage, union, and multi-state tax compliance
- [Field Operations](https://www.miter.com/): Time tracking, scheduling, daily reports, production tracking, safety
- [Expense Management](https://www.miter.com/): Reimbursements, per diems, corporate cards

## API
- [Miter REST API](https://www.miter.com/integrations/): REST API for building custom integrations between Miter and construction ERP/accounting/workforce systems. Auth-gated (api.miter.com); no public developer portal or OpenAPI definition is published.
- [Integrations](https://www.miter.com/integrations/): Off-the-shelf connectors for Sage Intacct, NetSuite, Acumatica, QuickBooks, Procore, Viewpoint, BuildOps, ServiceTitan, HCSS, BambooHR, Checkr, Indeed, LinkedIn, and more
